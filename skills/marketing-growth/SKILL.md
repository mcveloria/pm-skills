# Marketing & Growth

Work the funnel in order of leverage. Fix the leakiest stage before pouring more traffic in.

## Framework: AARRR + loops

| Stage | Question | Example metrics |
|---|---|---|
| Acquisition | Where do the right users come from? | Visitors, CAC by channel, signup rate |
| Activation | Do they reach the "aha" fast? | % reaching activation event, time-to-value |
| Retention | Do they come back? | D1/D7/D30, weekly active, cohort curves |
| Revenue | Do they pay and expand? | Conversion to paid, ARPU, NRR, LTV:CAC |
| Referral | Do they bring others? | Invite rate, K-factor |

Prefer **growth loops** (output of one cycle feeds the next: content → search traffic → signups → more content) over linear funnels where possible.

## Workflow

1. **Diagnose.** Get the current funnel numbers by stage and channel. Identify the biggest drop-off and the best-retaining cohort.
2. **Define the activation event** (the action that best predicts retention). Validate with cohort comparison.
3. **Choose channels** with the Bullseye method: brainstorm all, pick top 3 by fit, test cheaply, double down on one. Check channel-product fit: price point and sales motion must support the CAC.
4. **Generate experiments** with ICE or RICE scoring.
5. **Run experiments** (template below), decide, log learnings.
6. **Build lifecycle messaging**: welcome, activation nudges, habit triggers, win-back, expansion.

## Experiment template

```
Hypothesis: If we [change], then [metric] will [direction, size], because [insight].
Primary metric / guardrail metrics:
Audience and sample size (power calc, MDE):
Duration (full weeks, avoid novelty effect):
Decision rule set in advance:
Result / learning / next step:
```

Sample size sanity check: for a 5% baseline conversion and a 20% relative lift at 95% confidence and 80% power, expect roughly 8,000+ users per variant. If traffic is too low, use bigger swings or qualitative tests.

## Channel playbooks (short)

- **SEO/content:** target intent, not volume. Cluster topics, build pillar pages, refresh winners, track conversions rather than traffic.
- **Paid:** start with one platform, one audience, three creatives; judge on payback period, not CTR.
- **Lifecycle/email:** segment by behavior; each message has one job and one CTA.
- **Referral/virality:** reward both sides, put the ask at a moment of delight.
- **Partnerships/community:** trade audience access for shared value; measure sourced pipeline.
- **Product-led:** free tier or trial, in-product prompts, usage-based triggers for upgrade.

## Retention levers

Onboarding checklist and templates, reminders tied to value, habit loops, resurrection campaigns, annual plan offers, cancellation-flow learning (ask why, offer pause).

## Unit economics checks

CAC payback under 12 months (under 6 for SMB self-serve), LTV:CAC above 3, gross margin accounted for. Report blended and by-channel numbers.

## Outputs

Growth model (spreadsheet outline), channel scorecard, experiment backlog with scores, onboarding redesign brief, lifecycle email map, weekly growth review.

## Pitfalls

Optimizing vanity metrics, testing without enough traffic, ignoring guardrails (revenue up, retention down), scaling a channel before retention is healthy.
