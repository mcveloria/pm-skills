# Go-to-Market

A GTM plan answers: who buys, why us, how much, how they find and buy it, and how we know it's working.

## Workflow

1. **Define ICP and buyers:** firmographics, trigger events, buying committee (user, champion, economic buyer, blocker). Prioritize one beachhead segment.
2. **Positioning and messaging:** category, value proposition, 3 message pillars with proof points, per-persona variants, objection handling.
3. **Pricing and packaging** (below).
4. **Choose the motion:** product-led, sales-led, partner-led, or hybrid. Match it to ACV: under ~$5k tends toward self-serve, $15-50k+ toward sales-assisted.
5. **Channel and demand plan:** where the ICP already spends attention; budget, target volumes, funnel math.
6. **Enablement:** sales deck, demo script, battlecards, FAQ, ROI calculator, training.
7. **Launch plan** by tier, then **measure and iterate**.

## Pricing and packaging

- Anchor on value metric (what scales with customer value: seats, usage, outcomes).
- Methods: cost-plus (floor), competitor-based (reference), value-based (target). Validate with Van Westendorp or Gabor-Granger surveys and live willingness-to-pay interviews.
- Good/Better/Best tiers; make the middle tier the target; gate by value, not by arbitrary limits.
- Decide free tier, trial, or demo; annual discount; enterprise custom pricing.
- Plan grandfathering and migration for existing customers.

## Launch tiers

| Tier | Scope | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | New product / major strategic | Press, event, full campaign, sales kickoff |
| 2 | Significant feature | Blog, email, in-app, webinar, enablement |
| 3 | Minor improvement | Release notes, in-app message, changelog |

## Launch checklist (T-minus)

- **T-8 weeks:** positioning locked, pricing approved, beta customers recruited
- **T-4:** assets drafted (site, deck, demo, docs), sales/CS training scheduled
- **T-2:** beta feedback applied, analytics verified, press/analyst briefings, readiness review
- **T-0:** announce across site, email, social, in-app, sales outreach, partners
- **T+1 to 4 weeks:** monitor funnel, collect feedback, fix friction, case studies, retrospective

## Metrics

Pipeline created, signups, activation rate, conversion, win rate, sales cycle length, CAC, payback, time-to-first-value, NPS, launch awareness (share of voice).

## Outputs

GTM plan, ICP and persona sheet, messaging house, pricing recommendation with options, launch calendar, battlecards, enablement kit outline, launch retro.

## Pitfalls

Launching to everyone instead of a beachhead, features-first messaging, pricing set last, sales and CS learning about the launch at announcement, no post-launch owner.
